Splatbox

Turn a photo and its depth map into Gaussian splats. Orbit them here, then export a .ply for a splat viewer.

Use the grayscale depth image from your ComfyUI workflow. White or black as near both work; the page guesses and you can flip it below.

Rough guess from the picture, no AI: lower in frame is nearer. Expect a flat, stage-set look.

Runs on your device with Depth Anything V2 Small. The first run downloads about 40 MB (model and runtime), then it is quick. If it fails, use a depth map.
