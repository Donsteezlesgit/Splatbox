# Test on your PC (beta, ~10 min)
Open the beta link. You should see an amber BETA badge.

1. Home -> Back up all. Check a `.splatbox` file downloads.
2. Open Splatbox (the real one), open a project, File > Export backup.
3. In BETA: import that file. Open the copy. It should look exactly like the original.
4. Click and drag: left-drag should look around, right is right. W/A/S/D fly at a steady speed.
5. Add a camera. Look through it. Leave it. The camera should stay where it was.
6. Turn on fog or defocus. Fly the camera. The depth effect should not jump.
7. Two overlapping splats: no popping front/back.
8. Change a few effects, press Save (Ctrl+S), reload the page, reopen. Everything as left.
9. Presets: in File, type a name and save a preset. Open another project, apply it. Looks and LUT should match.
10. Anything slow, glitchy or odd? Tell me what you did just before.

All good? Say "promote".
